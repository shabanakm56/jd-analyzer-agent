Job Summary
We are seeking a skilled and experienced AR/VR Developer to build AI Products. The ideal candidate will have a deep understanding of augmented reality, 3D modeling, and computer vision to create a realistic and immersive try-on experience for our customers. You will be responsible for the full development lifecycle, from concept to deployment, working to integrate our product catalog with the virtual try-on feature.

Roles and Responsibilities
Develop and maintain AI models using ComfyUI. VTON
Work with Stable Diffusion for image generation tasks.
Design, develop, and maintain our AI Products for mobile and/or web platforms.
Implement augmented reality functionality using frameworks such as ARKit and ARCore.
Collaborate with designers and 3D artists to create and optimize photorealistic 3D product models for real-time rendering.
Utilize computer vision and machine learning techniques for accurate facial and body tracking to ensure precise placement of virtual products.
Integrate the virtual try-on feature with our existing e-commerce platform and product catalog APIs.
Optimize the application for performance, quality, and responsiveness on various devices.
Conduct thorough testing and troubleshooting to ensure a seamless and bug-free user experience.
Stay up-to-date with the latest AR/VR technologies and industry trends to continuously improve our product.
Required Skills and Qualifications
Proven experience developing AR/VR applications, preferably with a focus on virtual try-on or similar e-commerce applications.
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, C# or C++.
Strong experience with game engines like Unity3D or Unreal Engine.
Solid understanding of 3D graphics, physics, and rendering principles.
Experience with computer vision libraries and machine learning frameworks.
Demonstrated ability to create and optimize 3D models and assets.
A portfolio showcasing relevant AR/VR projects.
Experience with ComfyUI and Stable Diffusion.
Strong skills in prompt engineering.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience with both iOS and Android mobile development.
Knowledge of WebAR.
Experience with generative AI for creating realistic product visualizations.
