Job description
We are hiring an Artificial Intelligence Engineer on behalf of our client. This role involves building intelligent systems and AI-powered solutions that solve real business problems, from machine learning models to generative AI applications. Its a great opportunity for someone passionate about AI who enjoys experimenting with new technologies and turning ideas into working products.


Role & responsibilities

Design, train, and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models
Build AI-powered applications using LLMs, NLP, and computer vision
Develop chatbots, AI agents, and automation solutions
Build RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines using vector databases and embeddings
Collect, clean, and prepare datasets for model training
Work with Generative AI tools and APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face)
Apply prompt engineering to improve model accuracy and reliability
Deploy AI models into production using APIs and cloud platforms
Monitor model performance and improve accuracy, speed, and cost
Collaborate with data scientists, engineers, and product teams to define AI use cases
Document models, experiments, and results clearly
Stay updated on the latest AI research, tools, and trends

0 to 2 years of experience in AI/ML or related role (internships and projects count)
Strong programming skills in Python
Good understanding of machine learning, deep learning, and statistics
Hands-on experience with TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn
Familiarity with NLP, LLMs, or computer vision
